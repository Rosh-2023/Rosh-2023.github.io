# Observation of planetary subsurfaces in microwaves: Investigation of the anomalous regions of Enceladus

**Author:** Salman Raza  
**Institution:** [Add institution / doctoral school]  
**Supervisors:** Alice Le Gall and Frédéric Schmidt  
**Defence:** 30 September 2026

## Abstract

This thesis investigates the anomalous regions of Enceladus using microwave remote sensing. It focuses on the joint interpretation of active and passive observations from the Cassini RADAR instrument, supported by electromagnetic and thermal modelling.

The objective is to constrain the physical properties of the icy regolith and investigate the subsurface thermal structure, with particular focus on the South Polar Terrain.
